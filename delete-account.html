How to request deletion

To request deletion of your account and associated data, send an email to:

homefixpro.offcl@gmail.com

Subject: Account Deletion Request — HomeFixPro

Include your registered phone number in the email.

What gets deleted

  • Your name and phone number
  • Your location data
  • Your service request history

Retention

Data will be permanently deleted within 7 days of receiving your request.
